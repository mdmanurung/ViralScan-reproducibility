## 2. HSV-1 dose-response (`cell_level_virus_rates.tsv`)

Cells with >=10 HSV-1 molecules among called cells:
- uninfected (5 libs): 0%.
- 1 hpi: 0 to 0.15%.
- 3 hpi: 0.69 to 1.1% (synchronous 4C protocol), 29 to 31% (non-synchronous 37C protocol).
- 5 hpi: 4.9 to 8.2% (synchronous), 59 to 69% (non-synchronous).
Time and dose rise monotonically within each protocol. This is the cleanest recovery figure and needs no extra run.

## 3. HHV-6B vs published super-expressor rate

Lareau 2023 reports super-expressors at about 0.01-0.3% of cells and 0.2% in D98. ViralScan (>=10 HHV-6B molecules among called cells, GEX libraries):
- GSM6416029 (D98-Day19): 0.305%. Paper: ~0.2% in D98. Concordant.
- GSM6416028 (D97-Day19): 0.012%. Paper range 0.01-0.3%. Concordant.
- GSM6416022 (PBMC day-15 infection, positive control): 99.99%.
- D34 libraries (GSM6416023, 024): 41% and 56%; GSM6416027 (D61): 45%. These MATCH the paper: at later time points in D34 and D61 the paper reports 49% and 62% of all T cells as super-expressors (preprint Fig. 2g). The paper's 0.01-0.3% figure applies to early or other-donor libraries (D98, D97, D34 day 19). Count is viral molecules in called cells, so heavy ambient can inflate it; it is not a separate infected-cell call.
- TCR (VDJ-enriched) libraries are not comparable and are listed separately in the table.
- GSM6416026 (D38-Day19): 0 cells, 1 molecule: no signal; GEO gives no infection label, so this is not a confirmed true negative.

## 4. Index-hopping / swap check (`swap/`)

Pools were inferred from flowcell + lane in the first read header of each library (`swap/flowcell_per_library_first_run.tsv`); SRA-stripped headers (hsv1, hhv6b, ebv) carry no flowcell, so those datasets cannot be pooled and are not assessed. A library spanning several runs is placed by its first run only (approximate).
- **hpv16: yes, swapping signature.** GSM5705759 (parental) has 16 non-called HPV16 barcodes, 12 of which are barcodes carrying HPV16 in GSM5705760 (expected 0.03, p=1e-30). The parental library's few HPV16 molecules are therefore consistent with barcode swapping from the HPV16 library, not a parental infection. Do not claim the parental library is "positive".
- **hnscc: Vaccinia barcodes are shared between same-flowcell library pairs far above chance** (e.g. GSM5017045/046: 30 vs 0.06 expected). This supports reading the vaccinia hits as carry-over/ambient, not infection. Descriptive only (tier 1); the test cannot tell index hopping from a shared barcode artefact.

## 5. HIV-1 in hhv6b is vector-like (`hiv1_vector_footprint.tsv`)

In the six libraries with HIV-1 `locus_restricted` support (GSM6416023, 024, 027, 028, 029 and the weaker 032) 0.0% to 0.02% of read depth falls in NC_001802.1 positions 700-7,100 (gag-pol-vif-vpr-tat region). Coverage is confined to 5' LTR R-U5 (~120-180), a ~330-670 packaging/gag segment, an env/RRE segment (~7,170-8,025), ~8,560-8,670 and 3' LTR R-U5 (~9,070-9,170). A replicating HIV-1 would cover the interior. This is the footprint of a lentiviral transfer vector (these are CAR-T products). Wording: "HIV-1-derived sequence consistent with the CAR lentiviral vector; not an HIV-1 infection". Coordinates are in NC_001802.1; annotation check against `Human_immunodeficiency_virus_1_NC_001802.gtf` still to cite feature-by-feature.

- EM does not collapse the sibling counts to zero: weighted counts cannot tell cross-mapping from a real sibling because EM only splits molecules that are compatible with both. The decision therefore rests on sibling-specific molecules (`sibling_specific.tsv`: HHV-6A 8-181 specific molecules, HSV-2 0), not on the weighted totals. Report specific-molecule counts and say the weighted totals are an upper bound.
- No pilot changes a tier or a conclusion. The expected viruses are unchanged to within 0.1-0.3% under all methods.
- EM for EBV GSM4796271 could not run: its raw `output.bus` was removed by the earlier manual EBV deletion (review finding, 2026-10-18).
- Vaccinia stays at 1 infected cell under every method.
